# Demand: Conversations Need a Host

> Great gatherings should not depend on one person to keep the conversation going.

- Parties and events lose momentum when guests talk over one another or stay in separate conversations.
- Hosts need to listen, recognize participants, and introduce the next engaging topic.
- Manual moderation is distracting and difficult to sustain.

**Demand:** People want connection—but conversations need active facilitation.

# Solution: Ice Breaker, Your AI Co-host

> An AI co-host that listens, understands, and keeps the room moving.

- Start a live conversation from the browser.
- Higgs Realtime listens and responds with voice.
- Chat history shows everything spoken and heard, with best-effort speaker labels.
- `party-host-agent.md` guides warm, inclusive activities and transitions.

**Flow:** Guests speak → Higgs listens → Ice Breaker identifies speakers → AI co-host responds → The next activity begins.
